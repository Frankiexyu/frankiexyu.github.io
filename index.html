About

I am a Senior Associate at the Bai Xian Asia Institute in Hong Kong. I hold a Master of Public Policy from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and a Bachelor of Management in Public Administration from Fudan University.

My work evaluates policy programmes such as emissions trading, air-pollution campaigns and the coal-to-gas transition, and asks how central–local relations and campaign-style governance shape their results. I am increasingly interested in China–Middle East energy relations.

Research

The emission-reduction effect of China's carbon trading market: an empirical analysis based on the synthetic control method

With Chen Xing. Fudan Public Administration Review, Vol. 26, pp. 207–240, 2023. In Chinese.

Finds that the more active regional ETS pilots (Hubei, Shenzhen, Guangdong) cut emissions significantly.

Does China's "coal-to-gas" policy boost natural gas consumption? Evidence from a PSM-DID design

Term paper, HKUST, 2024.

Across 281 cities, gas consumption in the treated cities fell by about 28%, pointing to supply and implementation failures.

Performance evaluation of collaborative air-pollution governance in China: the Blue-Sky Protection Campaign

Undergraduate thesis, Fudan University, 2022. Supervised by Zhou Lingyi.

Synthetic DID on 240 cities shows PM2.5 reductions were largest where central intervention was strongest.

ESG compliance and regulatory gaps: strengthening Hong Kong listed companies' ESG reporting capacity

Policy Analysis Project for Alaya Consulting, HKUST, 2024. Supervised by Prof. Masaru Yarime.

Did the Covid-19 pandemic affect people's belief in hard work? Evidence from six cities in Guangdong

Course paper, HKUST, 2023.
